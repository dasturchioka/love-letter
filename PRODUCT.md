## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite, explicitly chosen by the user. Deployment target is undecided.

## Users

One recipient, Shahlo: the user's friend's romantic interest. She opens the website on her phone using a QR code given to her.

## Product Purpose

A personal English letter that apologizes for an earlier rude message, expresses the sender's feelings, respects Shahlo's stated wish not to enter a relationship, and leaves an optional way to reply on Telegram. The user supplied the exact three paragraphs.

## Operating Context

The QR code opens a publicly reachable website. Local development is used to preview and choose ideas with Impeccable live mode. The primary viewing context is mobile web; the specific phone and browser are unknown.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two states in one page: a title-only opening, then the full English letter.
- The opening title is exactly “What I want to say…” and acts as the touch/keyboard opening control. There is no small opening copy.
- Three user-supplied paragraphs reveal sequentially; each waits for the previous reveal to finish.
- The final action is “Write your answer...” linking to `https://t.me/xusainov055`.
- The former affirmative/negative controls, evasive notes and acceptance screen have been removed.
- The previously requested “From Terrorist” signature and its fingerprint-heart sticker sit inside the letter's footer.
- The user will choose the music. No music file has been supplied yet.
- Mobile browser audio starts after a user gesture; the opening interaction can supply that gesture.
- Confirmed in live: remove the top wordmark and all visitor-facing music controls. The recipient cannot adjust volume, pause, or toggle music within the page. Playback begins with opening the letter and loops at a fixed code-defined volume.
- Final music, optional personal imagery and the public hosting/QR destination remain undecided.
- The user explicitly requested design ideas and Impeccable live mode.
- Confirmed animation revision: animate the uploaded stickers around the letter, add stage transitions and faded typing to all visible text. The user provides visual feedback personally.

## Brand Commitments

All visitor-facing copy is English, including document language, metadata, accessibility labels and audio notices. Preserve the user-supplied paragraphs verbatim. No yes/no prompt or third state.

## Evidence on Hand

No personal photograph, GIF or audio has been supplied. Do not add invented personal history or promises to the supplied copy.

## Product Principles

- Read naturally as one person's message to another.
- Put mobile reading and touch interaction first.
- Preserve the letter's sincerity, readable pacing and optional reply action.
- Keep chosen music and personal material straightforward to replace.
